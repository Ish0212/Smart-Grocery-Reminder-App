Privacy Policy

Smart Grocery Reminder · Last updated 3 October 2026

Smart Grocery Reminder ("the app") helps you keep a grocery list and reminds you when you arrive at a saved store or get home. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, display name and a user ID, used to sign you in (Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Your list: the items you add, with optional aisle and notes, stored in Google Cloud Firestore so they sync between your devices.
  • Saved places: the name and coordinates of the home and stores you save, so the app can set reminders for them.
  • Preferences: your reminder settings.

Receipt photos

If you scan a receipt, the photo is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. It is not uploaded, stored, or shared, and prices aren't kept. Only the items you choose to add or check off go into your grocery list.

Location

The app uses iOS region monitoring to notice when you enter a saved store or arrive home. This happens on your iPhone. Your live location is not sent to us, stored, or shared. When you search for a store or address, the search goes to Apple Maps with an approximate area around you; Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

Shared lists

If you share a list or join someone else's, every member of that list can see and edit its items, see the user IDs of its members, and see who checked an item off. Your saved places, preferences and reminders stay private.

On-device only

Reminder statistics and the diagnostics log stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data with third parties. The only service providers are Google Firebase (sign-in and storage) and Apple (maps search). Firebase may collect basic diagnostic data to run its service.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your list, items, saved places, preferences and invite codes, and then the account itself. If you shared your list, it is deleted for the people you shared it with too; if you joined someone else's list, you leave it and it stays with its owner. The reminder statistics and diagnostics log on your iPhone are cleared as well.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
