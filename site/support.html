Common questions

I don't get a reminder at the store or at home. Open Settings in iOS → Smart Grocery → Location and choose Always, and allow notifications. In the app, Settings → Diagnostics shows a checklist of what's missing.

How do I share my list? Settings → Shared list → Invite someone. They enter the code under "Join a list with a code". Codes last 7 days.

How many stores can I save? Up to 19 stores plus your home (iOS allows 20 monitored places per app).

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a link to choose a new one.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete Account, then enter your password. All your data is removed.
